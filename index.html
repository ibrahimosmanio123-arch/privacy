Privacy Policy — Robo Vanguard

Vortex Anvil Games · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Robo Vanguard (“the game”) is published by Vortex Anvil Games (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game.

Information the game stores

Your progress (robots, upgrades, gold, settings) is saved only on your device. The game has no account system, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos, files or precise location. We do not run our own servers that receive your data.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play tells the game only which item was bought so it can be delivered.

Crash and performance information

Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash reports and performance statistics as part of the normal Play Console service. These do not identify you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Because progress is stored only on your device, uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it. The pause menu also has “Erase all progress”. Advertising data held by Google is managed through your Google account and device settings.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
